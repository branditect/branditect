About Branditect

A brand is not a logo.
It is everything you know
about your business.

Branditect is the commercial brain for product and ecommerce brands. It holds your strategy, your product truth and your margins in one place, so that everything you publish is on brand, accurate and profitable. Not a copy generator. A place your business thinks from.

Home. Brand Readiness tells you what is still missing, and the Studio row is what you can make with what the brain already knows. Nothing here is a guess.

Why we built it

Everything a brand knows is already written down. It is just written down in twelve different places.

Nobody is missing the information. They are missing one place that holds it, keeps it current, and can act on it. So the same three questions get asked forever.

“What do we actually stand for?”

The positioning was agreed in a workshop two years ago. It lives in a deck nobody has opened since, so every new person invents their own version of it.

Somewhere in a slide deck

“Can we honestly claim that?”

The test result exists. It is in a PDF from a lab, in an inbox. So the caption says “the best on the market” instead of the number that would have been better.

Somewhere in an inbox

“Can we afford this discount?”

The real margin is in a spreadsheet on one laptop. So the campaign goes out at minus thirty percent and the answer arrives a month later.

What it holds

Three kinds of truth, in one layer

Most tools know how to write. Branditect knows what is true about your business, and writes from that. It is the difference between content that sounds fine and content you can actually publish.

01

Brand truth

Who you are and what you stand against. Positioning, the difference nobody else can claim, your pillars and the proof beneath them, your tone of voice and the register you never use.

Everything is on brand

Product truth

Landed cost, tax, real margin, your floor price and the deepest discount you can survive. Held per product, because a six euro clip cannot carry a ninety nine euro floor.

Product truth and commercial truth in one row. Cost, price and real margin sit beside every product, and the asterisk is the system telling you a figure is estimated because a landed cost is missing. It would rather admit that than quietly overstate your margin.

What makes it different

Four decisions we will not trade away

It works closed book

It answers from what you gave it, not from the open internet. If the fact is not in your Knowledge, it says so instead of inventing something plausible. A confident wrong answer is worse than no answer.

Every claim shows its source

A number in a caption carries the document it came from. You can check it in one click. An unsourced figure is the failure this whole system exists to prevent.

It knows what you can afford

Margins are calculated net of tax against landed cost, and floor prices are set per product. No other brand tool will stop you writing an offer that loses money.

Your brand stays yours

Build the whole brain free and keep it readable forever. Export everything in one click, on every plan. A product that holds your brand hostage is not one you should trust with it.

Numbers. Work out what a sale really costs, what price hits the margin you want, and how deep a discount can go before it stops being worth running. The answers become the guardrails every piece of copy obeys.

Built for people who sell things they can hold.

It is for you if

  • You sell physical products, online or through retail
  • You are the founder, and also the marketing department
  • You know your margins matter and you keep them in a spreadsheet
  • You have a brand guideline that nobody reads
  • You run more than one brand, or you look after clients

It is not for you if

  • You want volume content with no particular brand behind it
  • You need invoicing, VAT returns or bookkeeping
  • You are looking for a stock image library
  • Your brand has no products and no numbers behind it
The company

Made in Finland, by people who have run the problem.

Branditect came out of agency work. The same pattern kept appearing: good brands with real products, losing consistency and margin not because they lacked talent, but because nothing held their decisions in one place. Every campaign started by reconstructing what had already been agreed.

So we built the thing we kept wishing existed. Not a faster way to write captions. A place where a brand's strategy, its product truth and its numbers live together, stay current, and can be acted on by anyone on the team without asking permission.

We are small, we are independent, and we build in the open with the people using it.
