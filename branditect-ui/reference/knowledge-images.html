Why the chips are on every tile

An image knowing which products it belongs to is what makes the library trustworthy. Without it you can see what a product has, but never what a file is for — and the only way to check your tagging is to open every product card in turn. The chips answer it at a glance, and removing one unlinks it without touching the file.
