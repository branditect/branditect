What this design commits to

Built from Studio's own swatches: navy, slate blue, purple, orange — the chips on the Home cards.

  • The hero states the product's central promise, not the page's name. "Your brand can only say what you've told it" is the reason Knowledge exists — a closed book that refuses to invent. That line does more for trust than any feature list, and it belongs on the screen where people decide whether to bother uploading.
  • Coverage is measured in areas fed, not files uploaded. "5 of 8 areas fed" with the missing ones as one-tap chips turns a vanity count into a to-do list. A user with 40 files and no pricing doc is worse off than one with 6 files covering everything, and only this framing shows it.
  • "Used in" is the column that changes behaviour. Seeing that the brand guideline fed 31 outputs is what makes someone upload the next file. A file library that never reports back feels like a filing cabinet; this makes it feel like an engine.
  • Three ways in, not one. Drop, write, link. Most brand knowledge isn't in a PDF — it's in someone's head or on their website, and a drop zone alone silently excludes both.
  • Status is honest and specific. Indexed, Indexing… with real progress, Needs a tag. The footer says what "needs a tag" costs: the file is read, but never cited by category. A warning without a consequence gets ignored.
  • Empty categories stay visible, dashed. Pricing at 0 is the most useful pill on the row — hiding it would hide the gap.
  • The hero gradient is made of the four chips from Home. Navy, slate blue, purple, orange — the exact swatches printed inside the Brand Assets and Do-the-numbers cards, run left to right. Nothing new was invented, and it lands on orange, so the page resolves into the accent rather than away from it.
  • Orange stays the action colour. The upload tile and the indexing bar are pure --grad-mark; the four-stop gradient is reserved for the hero and the active filter. If orange marked both "do this" and "this is a surface", it would stop meaning either.
