What changed

LayoutHome's structure, applied

Page title, then an orange hero beside a white panel at the same 1.55 / 1 split as Home, then a section heading, then cards. Five zones instead of eight — the flow strip folded into the card states, where it was always more useful.

LayoutThe setup is a panel, not a row of loose toggles

Three groups inside one bordered card with its own heading, exactly like "What's next". The read-back sentence sits at the bottom of the panel where it summarises rather than floats.

LayoutButtons line up

Every card's state line and button sit in a footer pinned with margin-top:auto. Descriptions can run different lengths and the row still reads as one horizon.

ArchitectureCalculators are a sandbox

They read from a product to prefill, and they never write back. One explicit handoff — Apply to product — opens the product card with the field filled in, for you to confirm and save. Prefill isn't a write.

ArchitectureThe product card is the live version

Which means no live/draft state machine and no "promote to live". Saved calculator runs are permanently what-ifs, because live isn't a state a scenario can be in. Your instinct made this simpler, not smaller.

Running costs stay shared

OpEx is one business-level set, not per product. Rent isn't a property of a hair dryer. Break-even is then computed against a product's contribution, or across the range.

The hero is the state, not the pitch

Home puts Brand Readiness in the orange. Here it's the equivalent: what's priced, what's weakest, what you must sell. The sentence explains what Numbers is; the tiles say where you stand.
