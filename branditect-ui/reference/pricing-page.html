Questions people ask

What happens when my 100 free credits run out?

You keep your account and everything in it. Your strategy, tone of voice, guidelines, products and anything you saved stay readable for as long as you want them. What stops is making new things. Nothing is deleted and you are never locked out of your own work. Upgrade whenever you are ready, or do not.

Do the free credits refill every month?

No. It is 100 credits once, and they do not expire. Use them over a weekend or over six months, whichever suits you. We would rather give you a real amount to try properly than a trickle that never lets you finish anything.

Is the whole brain really free to build?

Yes, and it stays free. The questionnaire, the strategy, your tone of voice, your products, your costs and margins, your indexed files and your Brand Readiness score cost nothing and are not time limited. Credits are only spent when the brain makes something. We think you will want that. If you do not, you still walk away with the brain.

What if I run out mid month on a paid plan?

We stop and tell you, rather than quietly billing you more. You can top up for €9 for 200 extra credits with one click, or wait for the next month. No surprise invoices, ever.

Do unused credits roll over?

No. Each month starts fresh. It keeps the price simple and it keeps us honest about what a plan is really worth to you.

Can I get my work out again?

Always, on every plan including Free. Your strategy, your files and everything Studio has written for you export in one click. Your brand belongs to you, and a product that holds it hostage is not one you should trust with it in the first place.

Is VAT included?

Prices shown include Finnish VAT at 25.5%. Businesses elsewhere in the EU with a valid VAT number are billed without VAT under the reverse charge, and you will see that at checkout. Outside the EU, no EU VAT applies.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from your settings, without emailing anyone. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for, and then your account continues as Free with everything still readable.

What counts as a brand?

One company, with its own strategy, voice, files and products. If you run two companies, or you are an agency looking after clients, each one is a separate brand and they never see each other. Pro Plus gives you three.
