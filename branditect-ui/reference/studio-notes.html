14-day copy plan — October

Day 1 · the problem, plainly

Sawdust was never a plan. It was just what was in the corner.

500ml. Soaks 1.2 litres. Sweeps up in one pass, and the floor is dry enough to work on.

Alternative opener, shorter: one bottle, one pass, dry floor. Test both on the Thursday post and see which one people actually reply to.

Day 2 · the number nobody believes
  • One bottle does what half a bag of granules does
  • Per litre absorbed we are cheaper — check the floor price before saying it out loud
  • Photograph the two side by side. The bag is the hero of that shot, not us.

Pour, sweep, done. No voiceover, just the sound of the shop.

What came out, and the one thing that stayed invisible

No source pills, no product chips, no voice panel. It is a document. Images sit in it at full width or half width with text beside them, and that is the whole layout vocabulary. Where a pinned line came from is still recorded, just not drawn — it costs nothing to store and it cannot be recovered later if it was never kept, so if you ever want to see it, it is a display change rather than a rebuild.
