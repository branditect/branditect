One message, twelve ways — and why the lines are ours

“Describe your tone of voice” is a blank page, and blank pages are where onboarding stops. Recognition is easy where description is hard. Holding the message constant is what makes the registers comparable: twelve unrelated slogans would mean reading twelve different things and inferring the difference, where twelve versions of one sentence show it. The lines are written for this screen rather than quoted, so nothing has to be sourced, nothing goes stale when a brand rebrands, and no other company’s copy lives permanently in the product. The brand names are comparators, in the smallest type on the card — you are choosing a register, not a brand to imitate.
