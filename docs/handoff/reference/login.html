Design notes

The tagline finally has a home

It was living in an in-app welcome modal that reopened on every page. A promise belongs where someone is deciding to come in, not on top of the workspace they already paid for.

Split, not centred

The old screen was a wordmark and two fields floating in white — it read as unfinished. The gradient panel carries the same hero treatment as Brand Readiness, so the product looks continuous from the first screen.

Six pills, and six is the ceiling

Each starts with what you do, not what the app has. Ordered as: what you don't have yet (strategy, voice), what you'll open it for weekly (copy, images), then the two things no rival can claim (margins, sourced answers). A seventh turns the set into feature soup.

Two states, one screen

Sign in and Create account share the layout and swap the panel. Click the tabs or the link below the form. Sign-up asks for brand name first — that's the thing the questionnaire needs.

Same tokens as Home

Plus Jakarta Sans, #f0562a, 46px controls, 12px field radius, 16px panels, tint-1 focus ring. Nothing new was invented for this screen.

Accessible by default

Real labels bound to inputs, visible focus rings, tabs with role="tablist", the password reveal announced, and the decorative peek card hidden from screen readers.
