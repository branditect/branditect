Design notes

AddedA margin column

You showed COGS and Price side by side and left the reader to do the arithmetic. Margin is the number that decides everything — it's the whole reason Numbers exists. Sortable, with the cash figure underneath.

AddedGuardrails in the Pricing tab

Floor price, deepest discount, minimum margin. This is the feature that lets you say "it won't write an offer that loses you money" — showing it on the product makes the promise concrete.

AddedA "use this product" row

Write about it · Make images · Ask about it. Without these, Products is a catalogue. With them, it's the thing that feeds Studio — which is the only reason a brand tool holds products at all.

Two margins, and the honest one wins

Factory cost gives 86.9%. Landed cost after VAT gives 82.2%. Show the second as the headline or the number flatters you, and a discount rule built on it will quietly eat the difference.

Inventory, trimmed on purpose

Kept: a stock status, so Studio won't promote what you can't ship. Dropped: reorder point, supplier, lead time. Those are ops fields — hold them here and stale data becomes a brand risk, and you're competing with Shopify instead of an agency.

Product images are placeholders

Tinted tiles with category glyphs stand in for your real photography. Everything else — type, colour, spacing, radii — is the shipped token set.
